# Payment logo sources

Retrieved 2026-10-18. The donation page uses local PNG images only.

- `visa.png`, `mastercard.png`: [Paytrail payment logos](https://www.paytrail.com/en/logos), from [card logo archive](https://www.paytrail.com/hubfs/Logot%20ja%20bannerit%202025/korttien%20logot%202025.zip).
- `applepay.png`: [Apple Pay mark hosted by Paytrail](https://www.paytrail.com/hubfs/Apple_Pay_Mark_RGB.png).
- `googlepay.png`: [Google Pay mark hosted by Paytrail](https://www.paytrail.com/hubfs/google-pay-mark.png).
- `revolut-pay.png`: [Revolut's official checkout example](https://github.com/revolut-engineering/revolut-checkout-example/blob/main/images/logo-revolut-pay.svg). White artwork displayed on a dark background.
- `link.png`: header logo from [Link's official website](https://www.link.com). Original paths and original green variant colors (`#011e0f`, `#00d66f`) preserved.
- `powered-by-stripe.png`: black badge from [Stripe's official media assets](https://stripe.com/newsroom/information), [badge archive](https://assets.stripeassets.com/fzn2n1nzq965/3lP8XFivEIUX19bLRZYqqW/efc69d1651371084eaa9ff404dbcc03c/Powered_by_Stripe-badge.zip).

Where supplied as SVG, the original artwork was rasterized to a 600-pixel-wide PNG using resvg, preserving proportions. Apple Pay and Google Pay were downloaded directly as PNG. No logos were hand-drawn. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.
